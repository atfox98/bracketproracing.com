Bracket Pro Timeslip Scanner Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

This policy covers the Bracket Pro Timeslip Scanner app for iPhone ("the app"), made by Bracket Pro Racing ("we"). In short: your timeslips go from your phone to your own computer, not to us. We have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your timeslips

The app reads timeslips on your phone. The text and photo of each slip are sent over Bluetooth to Bracket Pro on the computer you paired the phone with, and nowhere else. Nothing is sent over the internet, and we never see your slips, runs or logbook.

The app keeps on your phone the name you give it, the pairing details for your computer, and a log of what it did, which you can read in Settings. Deleting the app deletes them.

Camera and Bluetooth

The app uses the camera to scan the pairing code in Bracket Pro and your timeslips, and Bluetooth to send them to your computer. If you choose a picture with Choose from Photos, the app only gets the picture you pick. You can turn these permissions off in iPhone Settings at any time.

Purchases

The full version is bought through Apple; we never see your payment details. To check whether this phone has the full version, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives a random ID made for this app (not your name or Apple Account), your purchase history in the app, and basic device details such as your iOS and app versions, country and IP address. It's used only to unlock and restore your purchase, never for advertising or tracking.

Reporting a misread

If you tap Report a misread, the app opens an email to us with the slip's photos, the text the app read, what Bracket Pro made of it, when it was scanned, your computer's name in the app, and the app's version. It's only sent if you choose to send it. We use these emails to answer you and to improve how slips are read, and we may keep the slip photos and text to test future versions. Email support@bracketproracing.com to have them deleted.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
