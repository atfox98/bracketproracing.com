Bracket Pro

A logbook, weather station and ET predictor for 1/8 and 1/4 mile bracket racing. Plug in a Bracket Pro weather station, record each run from your timeslip, and Bracket Pro tells you what your car should run in the air you're racing in right now.

Weather, every minute

The Bracket Pro weather station plugs into your laptop by USB. Bracket Pro reads it every 6 seconds and saves a reading every minute: temperature, humidity, pressure, and the values worked out from them, such as density altitude, air density, dew point and vapor pressure, at the elevation of the track you're at. Each value shows how much it has changed since your last run.

Graphs show any value from the last 15 minutes to 72 hours, with your runs marked on them.

Your logbook

Run Entry is laid out like a timeslip: reaction, 60 ft, 330 ft, 594 ft, ET and MPH, then the dial-in, with the splits worked out as you type. Each run keeps the weather it was made in. 1/4 mile tracks add the 1000 ft and 1/4 mile ET and MPH. Or skip the typing: the Timeslip Scanner app fills it in from a photo of the slip.

Predictions

Predictions are only as good as the runs they learn from. Run Selection picks them: by car, track, lane, time of day, recent runs, similar weather, similar ET, or one at a time. Every run shows whether it's used, and if not, why.

Predict shows three formulas side by side and their average, updated every time a minute of weather is saved. Each shows how well it fits your runs (R² and typical error) and a chart of the runs and the formula, so you can see which suits your car.

Formulas fit your runs against one or two conditions: curve and surface fits, weighted and planar fits, a rule of thumb from your last run at your own rates, and machine learning that says how close the next run is likely to be. Advanced users can write their own in Python.

Pager

With a pager transmitter, Bracket Pro sends the latest predictions, weather and your last run to your crew's pagers, or to yours in the staging lanes, every few minutes. You choose what each line shows.

What you need

  • A computer running Windows 10 or 11 or macOS 11 or later, with a screen at least 1280 × 720.
  • A Bracket Pro weather station (version 3) and its USB cable.
  • Optional: a pager transmitter and pagers.
  • Optional: an iPhone with the Timeslip Scanner app, and Bluetooth on the computer.

Coming from Bracket Pro 1? Your runs, tracks, formulas and pager numbers come with you.
