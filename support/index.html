Support

Email support@bracketproracing.com with any question about Bracket Pro or the Timeslip Scanner app. Say which computer and phone you use, and what you see.

The full version

How do I get the full version on a new or reset iPhone?
Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app and tap Restore purchase. You don't pay again.
How do I get a refund?
Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are too: go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

When the scanner doesn't connect

Bracket Pro says Bluetooth is unavailable.
Usually Bluetooth is off on the computer, or Bracket Pro isn't allowed to use it. On a Mac, allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth, then turn Settings → Timeslip scanner off and on. On Windows, check Settings → Privacy & security → Radios.
Bracket Pro says this computer's Bluetooth can't accept phones.
Some older Bluetooth adapters can only connect to things, not let phones connect to them. A small USB Bluetooth 5 adapter fixes it. Windows uses one Bluetooth adapter at a time, so after plugging one in, turn off the built-in one: in Device Manager, under Bluetooth, right-click it and choose Disable device, then unplug the new adapter and plug it back in.
Bracket Pro says Windows wouldn't let phones find this computer.
Restart the computer, then try again.
The phone can't find the computer.
Check that Bracket Pro is running with Settings → Timeslip scanner on, that Bluetooth is on for both, and that the phone is close by (Bluetooth reaches about 30 feet, less through a trailer wall). Keep the app open while you scan.
The phone says it was unpaired.
It was unpaired in Bracket Pro. Scan the pairing code again.
The phone says to update.
The phone app and Bracket Pro are from different generations. Update whichever is older.
A slip was read wrong, or the lane is wrong.
Fix the run in Run Entry before saving: the amber outlines show what to check. Then tap Report a misread under that slip in the phone app, add what was wrong and send the email.
